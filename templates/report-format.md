# Report format

How a direct report briefs a manager: short, ordered by what the reader must decide. Hourly or short reports use the same structure, shortened.

1. **BLUF** (bottom line up front): one or two sentences.
2. **Status (RAG):** Green, Amber or Red, with a trend (up, flat, down).
3. **KPIs:** value, target, trend. `UNKNOWN` when unmeasured; never invented.
4. **Delivered:** what was done, with the evidence (command output, link, file).
5. **RAID:** risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies. Each with severity, owner, mitigation, date.
6. **Decisions required:** start with open items from `decision-register.md` with their age, then new ones with options, a recommendation, a deadline and the cost of delay.
7. **Next period:** what happens next.
8. **Learning note:** one industry term or practice used in the report.

Rules: say what it is, why it matters and what it is for in plain words; say what was not tested; no history recap and no narrating successful output.
