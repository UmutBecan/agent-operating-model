# Variant: research and analysis

Append to `CLAUDE.md`.

## Rules
- Every claim has a source or is marked as the author's estimate. A number without a source is `UNKNOWN`.
- Quote at most a short phrase and attribute it; summarize in your own words.
- Check each source's licence before reusing code or text; copy ideas, not code, from restricted licences.
- Dated figures carry their date. Star counts, prices and rankings change.
- A verifier role re-opens the sources of any number the report depends on.

## Output
Lead with the conclusion, then the evidence, then what was not checked.
